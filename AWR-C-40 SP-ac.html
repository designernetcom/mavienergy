    AWR-C-40 SP (Single Phase Input Current up to 32 Amps) (AC type)


  • The AWR-C-40-SP is a single-phase solid-state relay (SSR) or AC power controller designed to manage AC loads with a maximum current rating of 40 amps. Below is a detailed breakdown of its specifications, features, and application information.

  • Electrical Specifications:

    Typically ranges from 4–32V DC or 90–280V AC depending on the control type.

    Common ratings are 240V AC or 480V AC, suitable for industrial power systems.

    Handles up to 40A continuous load current.

    Typically rated for 47–63 Hz, compatible with standard AC power grids.

  • Protection Features:

    Protects the relay and connected equipment from voltage spikes.

    May require external fuses or circuit breakers for optimal safety.

    Designed to handle short-term, high-inrush currents without damage.

    Requires appropriate heatsink installation to manage heat dissipation during continuous full load conditions.

  • Applications:

    Industrial Heating Systems: Accurate control for heating elements in ovens, furnaces, and plastic molding machines.

    Lighting Systems: Efficient switching of high-power lighting circuits.

    HVAC Systems: Effective for fans, compressors, and pumps.

    Machinery and Automation: Controls resistive, inductive, or capacitive loads in industrial settings.

    Power Supply Regulation: Maintains stable voltage control for sensitive equipment.
